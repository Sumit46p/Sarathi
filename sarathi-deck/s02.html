Problem Statement

Manual vehicle dispatch often relies on proximity and human judgment instead of actual road conditions and vehicle availability.

Real-world problem: The vehicle that appears closest may be unavailable, blocked, unsuitable, or much farther by road. Dispatchers must make these decisions manually, often through calls and incomplete information.
📏 Distance ambiguity
Straight-line distance ≠ actual road distance
🚑 Availability uncertainty
Nearby vehicles may be unavailable or blocked
📞 Manual coordination
Communication gaps slow dispatch decisions
